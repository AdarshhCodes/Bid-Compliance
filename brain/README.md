## 1. What is PRAMAAN?

**PRAMAAN (प्रमाण — Sanskrit for "evidence, proof, means of valid knowledge")** is an AI-assisted, evidence-first bid compliance verification and decision-support platform designed specifically for public procurement on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) and CPSE (Central Public Sector Enterprise) tenders such as those of CPCL.

PRAMAAN rejects the standard hackathon trope of an opaque 0–100% "compliance score" generated by an unverified Large Language Model (LLM). Instead, it transforms bid compliance evaluation from an exhausting document-reading exercise into a transparent, investigative, evidence-verification workflow:
- Every tender clause and bidder claim is anchored to a traceable, immutable **Evidence Record** citing exact document pages, bounding boxes, or registry responses.
- Every check resolves into an uncertainty-aware **Three-State Verdict**: `VERIFIED`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `UNVERIFIABLE`.
- It tracks cross-document discrepancies within a single bid, exposes cross-bidder collusion and relationship signals (shared bank accounts, shared contact info, near-identical technical boilerplate) across an entire tender, and commits every finding and officer review into a **tamper-evident decision audit ledger**.
- **Fundamental Operational Tenet:** PRAMAAN is a **decision-support system**, NOT an autonomous decision maker. Procurement officers retain statutory accountability; AI extracts, cross-checks, and surfaces evidence, but the officer makes the final decision.
